## Описание (вставьте в проект)
My personal portfolio website: an interactive, scroll-driven page that introduces my work as a web and UI/UX designer.

**Concept.** The page opens on a small desk scene with a character who greets the visitor and points to the work. As you scroll, the character follows the scroll position, the camera moves toward the monitor, and the monitor reveals the list of projects. From there the page continues into skills, an about section, five projects with full case studies, and contact links.

**What is on the site**
- A hero with a 3D desk scene and a scroll-controlled character animation
- Running lines with the four specialisations and the main tools
- An about section with a short statement and abstract line artwork
- Five projects (TRIPLY, LUMIÉ, MUZA, FlowAutomate, GIFT MATCH), each with a case study page and gallery
- Contact links: email, LinkedIn, Behance

**Design.** A dark interface with a violet and copper accent on near-black (#0C0C0C), light blue-grey text (#D7E2EA) and the Kanit typeface. Large metallic headlines, rounded cards and pill buttons keep the page bold and calm.

**Responsive and accessible.** The page has desktop, tablet and phone layouts. On phones and devices without WebGL the 3D scene is replaced by a lighter HTML version; for visitors who prefer reduced motion, the animation is replaced by a still image.
