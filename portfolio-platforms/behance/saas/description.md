## Short description
A dark landing page for a 14-day AI-automation sprint: a four-step "What you'll learn", outcomes, three pricing tiers and an FAQ.

## Project overview
A dark, high-contrast landing page for an AI automation offer: "Automate Your Income with AI", a 14-day sprint that teaches workflow automation with no coding required. The page in these captures is branded Flowcore.

## Brief and goals
Explain the offer and convert: a hero with two actions, a four-step "What you'll learn", outcomes, three pricing tiers, an FAQ and a final sign-up.

## Audience
People who want to automate tasks and build income-generating workflows with AI, without coding. The copy says "No coding required" and the FAQ opens with "Do I need coding skills?"

## Design challenge
Make a technical offer feel approachable: show what the sprint teaches in plain steps, and make "no coding required" believable.

## Process
A single landing page, top to bottom:
- Hero: headline, "Get in touch" and "View services"
- Inside the Sprint: four steps with interface previews
- What you'll achieve: four benefit cards
- Pricing: Free, Pro and Premium with a monthly and annual toggle
- FAQ, a "Build Your First System" sign-up and a footer

## Key design decisions
- One purple accent on near-black marks every action: Join Program, Get in touch, Get Access
- Four numbered steps, each with a small interface preview, explain the sprint at a glance
- Pricing has a highlighted "Pro" plan marked Best and a billing toggle
- An FAQ accordion answers the main objections: coding skills, duration, real projects, work use, subscription

## Visual design
A near-black page with a deep purple glow and one violet accent colour.
- Satoshi for headings, Inter for text
- Rounded dark cards with thin borders
